Understanding Your Metrics

Average Stability: Session-wide balance consistency. Percentage of frames where your center of mass stays within the optimal balance zone throughout the entire climb.
Frame Stability: Current position accuracy. Real-time score showing how close your body position is to the ideal balance point.
Movement Velocity: Speed of body movement. Measures how fast your center of mass moves between frames. Lower variance indicates smoother technique.
